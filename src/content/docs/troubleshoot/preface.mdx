:::caution[Before we begin, ensure the following things]
- You must have the latest version of Fishstrap installed
- Your internet connection is stable and working properly
- Your device meets the minimum system requirements for running Roblox and
  Fishstrap (Windows 10 and later)
- Roblox is not down for maintenance (there's nothing we can do)
- You are not running any exploits (no support will be provided for you)
:::
