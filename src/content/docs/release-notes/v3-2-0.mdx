## Additions

### VNGGames client support

Fishstrap now supports VNGGames client distribution for our Vietnamese users!

![](../../../assets/img/release-notes/v3-2-0_vngclient.png)

:::caution[On a side note...]
**Using it outside of Vietnam is not recommended** as agreeing to VNGGames'
terms could cause irreversible changes to your Roblox account.
:::

### New Integrations options

We added two new options to the Integrations page: you can now toggle Roblox
running in the background and choose between light and dark app themes.

![](../../../assets/img/release-notes/v3-2-0_integrations.png)
